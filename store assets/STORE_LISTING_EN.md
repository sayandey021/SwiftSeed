### Experience Fast & Secure Torrenting with SwiftSeed v2.0

SwiftSeed is a modern, powerful, and privacy-focused torrent search and download manager for Windows. Designed with speed, security, and user experience in mind, SwiftSeed brings you the ultimate torrenting experience in one beautiful application.

### 🔍 Unified Multi-Provider Search
Search across multiple torrent providers simultaneously with lightning-fast results:
• ThePirateBay - The world's most resilient torrent site
• Nyaa - Premier anime and Asian content tracker
• 1337x - High-quality torrents across all categories
• TorrentsCSV - Community-driven torrent index
• Custom Torznab Providers - Add your own indexers

Get results in milliseconds with our optimized aggregation engine that searches all providers at once, saving you valuable time.

### 🚀 Integrated Download Manager
No need for multiple applications! SwiftSeed includes a powerful built-in torrent downloader:
• Powered by libtorrent for maximum performance
• Real-time download progress and speed monitoring
• Pause, resume, and stop downloads with ease
• Sequential downloading for media files
• File priority management
• Speed limits and bandwidth control
• Automatic error recovery

### 🎨 Modern & Beautiful Interface
Enjoy a premium user experience with our sleek, modern design:
• Beautiful dark and light themes
• Smooth animations and transitions
• Intuitive, user-friendly layout
• Responsive design that adapts to your workflow
• Material Design principles
• Clean, distraction-free interface

### 📂 Smart Organization & Filtering
Find exactly what you're looking for with powerful filtering:
• Category-based browsing (Movies, TV Shows, Games, Anime, Music, Apps, Books, etc.)
• Sort by seeders, size, or upload date
• Advanced search filters
• Multi-file torrent selection
• Choose specific files before downloading

### ⭐ Bookmarks & History
Never lose track of great content:
• Bookmark your favorite torrents for quick access
• Automatic search history tracking
• Easy management and cleanup
• Quick re-search from history

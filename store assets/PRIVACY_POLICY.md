# Privacy Policy for SwiftSeed

**Last Updated: December 4, 2025**

## Introduction

SwiftSeed ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SwiftSeed handles your information when you use our torrent search and download manager application.

## Our Privacy Commitment

**SwiftSeed is privacy-first by design. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data.**

## Information We Don't Collect

SwiftSeed does NOT collect:
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Search queries or search history (beyond local storage)
- Downloaded content information
- Device identifiers
- IP addresses
- Cookies or tracking data
- Any other personal or identifiable information

## Local Data Storage

SwiftSeed stores the following data ONLY on your local device:
- **Application Settings**: Your preferences and configuration
- **Search History**: Your search queries (stored locally, never transmitted)
- **Bookmarks**: Torrents you've bookmarked
- **Download State**: Information about your active and completed downloads
- **Custom Providers**: Custom torrent indexers you've added

All this data is stored in:
- **Windows**: `C:\Users\<YourUsername>\.swiftseed\`

This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not shared with any third party
- Can be deleted at any time by removing the folder

## Third-Party Services

SwiftSeed connects to third-party torrent indexing services to perform searches:
- ThePirateBay
- Nyaa
- 1337x
- TorrentsCSV
- Custom Torznab providers (if configured by you)

When you perform a search:
- Your search query is sent to these services
- These services may have their own privacy policies
- We do not control or track these interactions
- No identification information is sent with search queries

## Torrent Downloads

When downloading torrents:
- SwiftSeed uses the BitTorrent protocol
- Your IP address is visible to other peers in the torrent swarm (this is how BitTorrent works)
- We recommend using a VPN if you want to hide your IP address
- SwiftSeed does not track or store information about your downloads

## Data Security

Since SwiftSeed doesn't collect or transmit your data:
- There are no servers to be breached
- Your data stays on your device under your control
- You can delete all app data at any time

## Children's Privacy

SwiftSeed does not knowingly collect information from anyone. The app is rated Mature 17+ due to access to user-generated content.
